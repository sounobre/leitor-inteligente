---
name: Fantasy trail translations
description: Editorial rule for generated terms in the fantasy learning trail.
---

Every generated Fantasy Trail term must receive a Portuguese gloss that reflects its grammatical template and context.

**Why:** A generic label hides the relationship between the expanded English phrase and its intended meaning, making the learning cards misleading.

**How to apply:** When adding term templates, add a matching Portuguese gloss template and test representative base and derived cards. Keep the examples original and spoiler-free.
